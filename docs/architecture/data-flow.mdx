# Data Flow & Technical Sequence

To achieve high-performance 3D modeling and real-time energy simulation, our data flow is divided into two main layers: the **Business Workflow** and the **Technical Execution**.

### 1. Business Workflow (Lead to Proposal)

This is the end-to-end journey of a solar project:

1. **Intake**: Customer address is captured and converted to GPS coordinates.
2. **Imagery Sync**: Coordinates trigger a request to Map APIs to fetch high-resolution satellite imagery.
3. **3D Design**: The designer models the roof and places panels in the 3D scene.
4. **Energy Processing**: The layout is analyzed to predict monthly and annual energy production (kWh).
5. **Proposal Generation**: Technical results are combined with financial data into a branded PDF.

### 2. Technical Execution (Hybrid Stack)

How our tech stack (Rust, Python, Vue, Three.js) handles the data in real-time:

- **Frontend (Vue + Three.js)**:
  When a panel is placed, Three.js calculates its 3D coordinates. Vue.js immediately updates the **Local State** using Pinia.
- **Computation (Rust Service)**:
  The layout JSON is sent to the **Rust-based Energy Engine**. Rust performs vector math for shading and irradiance in microseconds.
- **AI Analysis (Python Service)**:
  The **Python AI module** processes satellite images in the background to detect roof planes and obstructions.
- **Database (Persistence)**:
  Final validated data is sent via the **Rust API** to be saved securely.

:::tip Performance Note
By offloading heavy math to **Rust** and AI to **Python**, the Vue/Three.js frontend remains smooth (60 FPS) even during complex simulations.
:::
